Plans that follow your screens, a clearer Displays page, and screens that are recognised for real.

### New

- **Plans that follow your screens.** For laptops: link a plan to each set of screens (**Settings → Plans → Plans that follow your screens**). Unplug the monitor and the open windows of your "laptop" plan go to their place; plug it back and your "desk" plan returns. Nothing is opened, minimized or brought to the front. Virtual screens (Parsec, Remote Desktop) do not count.
- **Screens are recognised by model and serial number**, not by the name Windows gives them. A monitor keeps its plans and spots whatever the port or dock, and two monitors of the same model stay apart.
- **Replaced a screen?** A new screen of the same model takes over the old one by itself. For a different model, the Displays page asks "Is this a new screen?": pick the one it replaces and its zones, plans and screen setups move over.
- **Confirmation before Replace and Clear** on a plan, saying exactly what will happen.

### Changed

- **Displays page redone.** Your screens are drawn where Windows places them; click one to see its zones and pick a layout next to it. Your own layouts are edited or deleted right on their tile. Screens show their real name ("Built-in display", "HP E24 G4").
