Maximize is yours again.

### Changed

- **The maximize button is a real maximize.** ZoneLock no longer pulls a maximized window back into a zone: maximizing a snapped window fills the screen, and restoring it puts it back in its zone. Zones are for snapping (Shift + drag, shortcuts, plans), and F11 fullscreen of a snapped window still stays in its zone.
