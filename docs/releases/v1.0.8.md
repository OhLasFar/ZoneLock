A zone editor that works like you expect: draw straight away, split with a click, move shared edges together, and see which apps will not fit.

### New

- **Draw my own opens the editor at once** on an empty screen. Press **Enter** when you are done and name your layout right there.
- **"+" buttons.** A big **+** in every empty spot fills it with a zone. Small **+** on each side of the zone under the mouse split it in two (left, right, top or bottom).
- **Shared edges move together.** Drag the line between zones and every zone along it follows, like a splitter. Hold **Ctrl** to move one zone only. Edges can be grabbed anywhere along their length.
- **Grid.** Press **G** (or the Grid button, bottom left) for a 4×4, 6×6, 8×8 or 12×12 grid; zone edges snap to it.
- **Zones too small for your apps.** ZoneLock asks the apps on your PC for their minimum size (Discord, Steam, Slack, Parsec…, even when they sit in the tray). In the editor, a zone that is too small gets an amber outline and the icon of each app that will not fit, crossed out; point at an icon to see why.
- **Force small zones** (Settings → General, off by default, marked *Risky*): squeezes apps below their own minimum size. ZoneLock asks before turning it on: apps can cut content, draw wrong or resize themselves back.

### Changed

- **Sharper editor.** Rounded zones, dots and buttons are smooth (anti-aliased) and the text has no more colour fringes.
- Each zone shows its number once, with its size in pixels next to it.
- The editor takes the keyboard when it opens, so **G**, **Enter** and **Esc** work right away.
