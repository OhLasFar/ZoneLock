Two new shortcuts, and a confirmation when an update is done.

### New

- **`Win+Ctrl+Alt+↑`: fullscreen in its zone, or on the whole screen.** A fullscreen window has no buttons: when a snapped app goes fullscreen inside its zone (Parsec connecting, a game, a video), this shortcut sends it to the whole screen, and the same keys bring it back into its zone. Leaving fullscreen puts it back in its place as usual.
- **`Win+Alt+H`: every shortcut at a glance.** A card in the middle of the screen lists all ZoneLock shortcuts with your current keys. Esc, the same keys or a click close it. It never takes the focus from the app you are in.
- **Update confirmation.** After an update, a card confirms that ZoneLock was updated and that your zones, plans and settings are kept.

Both shortcuts can be changed in **Settings → Shortcuts**.
