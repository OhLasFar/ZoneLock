Updates in one click, and fullscreen that respects windows you moved out of their zone.

### New

- **Updates**: ZoneLock checks GitHub once a day and shows a notification when a new version is out. **Settings → System → Updates** shows your version, a **Check now** button and **Install** when an update is available. The download is verified against the release's SHA-256 checksums, installed silently, and ZoneLock restarts by itself. The daily check can be turned off; it is ZoneLock's only network use and sends nothing about you.

### Fixed

- **Fullscreen of a window outside a zone gets the whole screen.** Only a window sitting in a zone keeps its fullscreen inside that zone. A free window going fullscreen (Parsec or Remote Desktop connecting, a game, a player you moved out of its zone) now covers the whole display, as it should.

### Upgrading from v1.0.0 to v1.0.2

Those versions cannot update themselves: download `zonelock-1.0.3-setup.exe` below and run it once. It installs over the old version and keeps your settings. From v1.0.3 on, updates come to you.
