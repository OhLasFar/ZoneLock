## What's new

- **First-run welcome card**: on first launch ZoneLock shows a native card with the current shortcuts and buttons to open Settings or dismiss. Re-open it any time from Settings > Maintenance.
- **Remappable drag-snap key**: the key held while dragging to show zones (default `Shift`) is now configurable in Settings > Shortcuts. Modifiers-only combos like `Ctrl+Alt` work, and clearing it shows the zones on every drag. The cheatsheet and the General tile reflect your choice.
- **Shortcut status**: the per-shortcut badge now only appears when the OS refused the key (already taken by another app) instead of always showing "Ready".
